Our Story

Two great cuisines.
One address.

Bambu Indian & Thai Restaurant has been serving the people of Limerick City with the finest Indian and Thai food for years. Located in the heart of Limerick City Centre at 28 O'Connell Street, we bring together two of the world's most beloved culinary traditions under one roof.

Our kitchen uses only 100% Halal-certified ingredients, sourced fresh daily. Whether you're craving a rich Chicken Tikka Masala, an aromatic Biryani, a fragrant Thai Green Curry, or a classic Pad Thai, Bambu is Limerick's destination for authentic Indian and Thai dining.

FAQs

Your Questions
Answered.

Is Bambu restaurant Halal in Limerick?

Yes. Bambu is a fully Halal-certified restaurant in Limerick. All our meat is sourced from certified Halal suppliers. We are one of Limerick City's top choices for authentic Halal Indian and Thai food.

Where is Bambu restaurant in Limerick?

Bambu is located at 28 O'Connell Street, Limerick City Centre — right in the heart of Limerick City, easily accessible on foot, by bus, or by car. Get directions →

What are Bambu's opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday: 17:00 – 22:45 and Sunday: 14:00 – 22:00. Open 7 days a week in Limerick City Centre.

Does Bambu serve Thai food in Limerick?

Yes! Bambu serves authentic Thai cuisine in Limerick including Pad Thai, Green Curry, Red Curry, Tom Yum Soup, Massaman Curry, and Thai Fried Rice — prepared with traditional Thai recipes.

What Indian food does Bambu serve?

Bambu serves authentic Indian food in Limerick — Chicken Tikka Masala, Biryani, Butter Chicken, Rogan Josh, Korma, Vindaloo, Balti, Tandoori specials, Onion Bhaji, and Samosas. All dishes are 100% Halal.

Can I book a table or order online?

Yes! Book a table online or call us on +353 61 217 661. Online ordering is also available at bambulimerick.ie/order-now.
